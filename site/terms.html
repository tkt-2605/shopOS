Using ShopOS

You may install ShopOS and use the Free plan in your business, on one counter, at no charge and for as long as you like. The Business and Cloud plans are sold separately and add more counters and services.

What you pay for

Business is a one-time payment for a licence that does not expire. Cloud is a monthly subscription. Prices are those agreed when you buy, and an invoice is issued for every payment.

If a subscription ends

Your shop can always bill. If a Cloud subscription ends and you do not hold a Business licence, ShopOS makes one final sync, then continues on the Free plan on one counter with all your data. Other counters stop taking new bills until the subscription is renewed or a Business licence is bought.

Updates are free on every plan. ShopOS offers them when your computer is online, and installs them only when you choose to.
