Returns in the GST export, over the owner's own database
========================================================

tools/fixtures/real-shop.db, pulled with `npm run pull-shop-db`, migrated to
head (28) on a throwaway copy. Figures printed by the report functions
themselves, not by hand-written SQL.

The fixture is gitignored real trade. These figures are here because the
assertions in reportsAgainstRealShop.test.ts are relative -- they say "agrees
with what the bills hold" -- and a relative assertion passes on an empty
database too. This is the absolute reading on the day the work was done.


Summary (whole history)
-----------------------

  bills   = 29
  total   = 27,328.00
  returns =    340.00
  net     = 26,988.00
  tax     =  3,137.77


Rate summary, after the change
------------------------------

  12%   taxable = 19,971.43   cgst = 1,198.21   sgst = 1,198.36   igst = 0.00
  18%   taxable =  3,915.23   cgst =   352.32   sgst =   352.45   igst = 0.00

Two things to read off this.

**The credit note is in it.** Before the change these rows were
19,971.43 + 303.57 of taxable value at 12%, declaring goods the shop had taken
back and handed the money for. The export now nets it.

**CGST and SGST still do not match.** 1,198.21 against 1,198.36 at 12%, and
352.32 against 352.45 at 18%. That is the bug fixed in
`8d55a02 CGST and SGST are the same number, and on 29 lines they were not`,
showing through in bills raised before the fix. It is not re-derived and must
not be: those bills were issued, the customer holds a copy, and a submitted
document is never rewritten. New bills split evenly; these stay as charged.


The one credit note the shop has raised
---------------------------------------

  credit_note  CRN/MR/26-27/00001
    against    INV/MR/26-27/00001
    dated      2026-09-12
    rate       12%
    taxable    -303.57
    tax         -36.43
    total      -340.00

39 rows in the export, 1 of them a credit note. It carries the invoice number
it reverses, which is Rule 53(1)(e) and which GSTR-1 table 9B is filled from.


Rounding
--------

The shop's `round_off_rule` is `none`, so every bill's rate rows already summed
to its grand total and the round_off change is a no-op here. It is proved on a
made-up bill instead, in `returnsInGst.test.ts` -- which is exactly the kind of
claim the fixture cannot check, and is said out loud rather than left implied.


One divergence this exposed, not fixed here
-------------------------------------------

`salesSummary.tax` is 3,137.77. The rate rows now come to 3,101.34. The
difference is 36.43, which is the tax on the credit note.

Both are defensible: the dashboard figure is tax charged on bills in the
period, the rate rows are what the return is filed on, which is net of credit
notes. They are not the same question. But they are two numbers on two screens
of one product that a shopkeeper will read as the same number, and the phase
checklist says `salesSummary` is to be left unchanged by this work, so it has
been. Recorded in findings-real-shop.md for the 1b/1c review to settle.
